# Publication Figures

This directory contains the high-resolution figures generated for the paper:
> **"The AI Divide and the Resilience Paradox: Digital Sanctions, Adaptive Agency, and Academic Tenacity among Iranian Scholars"**

All figures are rendered at 300 DPI (suitable for print and camera-ready submission) using Python (`python/06_publication_plots.py`) and R (`r/02_visualizations_publication.R`).

---

## Figure List & Captions

### Figure 1: Bivariate Correlation Heatmap
- **File:** `fig1_correlation_matrix.png`
- **Generated by:** `python/03_correlation_and_collinearity.py` / `python/06_publication_plots.py`
- **Caption:** Heatmap of Pearson correlation coefficients across primary study variables: Access Tiers (Tier 1 Free vs. Tier 2 Plus), Digital Sanctions Impact, AI Anxiety, Academic Resilience, and GPA. Significant at *p* < 0.001.

### Figure 2: The Negative Gradient of Privileged Access on GPA
- **File:** `fig2_access_vs_gpa_scatter.png`
- **Generated by:** `python/06_publication_plots.py`
- **Caption:** Scatter plot with fitted OLS regression line demonstrating the counter-intuitive negative association between unconstrained AI access (Tier 2) and academic achievement (GPA) ($r = -0.8086$).

### Figure 3: Resilience Paradox Across Access Tiers
- **File:** `fig3_resilience_paradox_distribution.png`
- **Generated by:** `r/02_visualizations_publication.R`
- **Caption:** Boxplot and jittered distribution contrasting Academic Tenacity and Resilience scores between sanctioned scholars (relying on workarounds) and unrestricted access users.

### Figure 4: Structural Mediation Path Model
- **File:** `fig4_mediation_model_path.png`
- **Generated by:** `r/03_sem_mediation_lavaan.R`
- **Caption:** Path diagram showing the direct and indirect effects of structural barriers on academic outcomes, mediated through compensatory resilience and agency (5,000 bootstrap iterations).
